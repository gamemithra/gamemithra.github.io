Parent and teacher guide

Ramayan Quest for Parents

A family-friendly Ramayana quiz and story adventure.

Ramayan Quest can be played as a shared discovery experience where children and adults encounter Ramayana-inspired characters, stories, places and values through quizzes and interactive scenes.

Trust Signals

What parents and educators should know

Age suitability

The game is positioned as family-friendly and suitable for younger players, students and culturally curious families.

Learning exposure

Players can encounter Ramayana stories, characters, values, places and cultural vocabulary through play.

Educational status

Ramayan Quest supports learning through quizzes and storytelling, but Gamemithra Studios does not claim formal educational accreditation.

Adult supervision

Adult supervision is optional and can make the experience more meaningful through conversation about the stories and questions.

Ads and privacy

The privacy policy says Gamemithra apps use AdMob and do not collect personally identifiable information from users.

Languages and offline play

Language support and offline requirements are not confirmed in the repository yet.
