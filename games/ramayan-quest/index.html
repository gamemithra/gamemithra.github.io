Ramayana quiz and story adventure

Ramayan Quest

A family-friendly Indian mythology game by Gamemithra Studios.

Gamemithra Studios develops and publishes Ramayan Quest, a mythology-inspired quiz and story adventure that helps players explore characters, stories, values and knowledge associated with the Ramayana through interactive play.

Questions

Clear answers for parents, teachers and players

What is Ramayan Quest?

Ramayan Quest is a Ramayana-inspired quiz and story adventure with interactive questions, story progression, characters and mythology-themed scenes.

Is Ramayan Quest suitable for children?

Ramayan Quest is positioned as a family-friendly title for younger players, families, students and culturally curious players.

Can parents play with children?

Yes. The quiz format and story moments are suitable for shared discovery and family conversation around Indian stories.

What can children learn?

Children can be exposed to Ramayana characters, places, values, stories and cultural vocabulary through play. No formal learning outcomes are claimed.

What languages are supported?

Language support is not confirmed in the repository. This page should be updated when the final in-game language list is verified.

Does it contain mature themes?

The game is positioned as family-friendly. The Ramayana includes battles and moral conflict, but the page does not claim mature-content ratings.
