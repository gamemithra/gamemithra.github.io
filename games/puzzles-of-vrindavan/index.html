Puzzles of Vrindavan

Gamemithra Studios develops Puzzles of Vrindavan, a playful puzzle adventure inspired by Krishna's Vrindavan, combining visual puzzles with light-hearted storytelling and exploration.

What is Puzzles of Vrindavan?

It is a playful puzzle adventure inspired by Krishna's Vrindavan, visual puzzle solving, symbols and light storytelling.

Is it suitable for children?

The game is positioned as family-friendly for children, families, puzzle players and culturally curious audiences.

What kind of puzzles are included?

The existing page describes rotating, placing and revealing puzzle tiles to uncover hidden symbols.

What is the inspiration?

The game draws from Krishna-themed art direction, Vrindavan-inspired settings, decorative Indian motifs and playful visual storytelling.
