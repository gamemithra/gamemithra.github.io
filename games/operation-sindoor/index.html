Operation Sindoor

An interactive story of courage, family, dreams and memory.

Gamemithra Studios develops Operation Sindoor, a cinematic visual novel blending emotional storytelling, educational themes, symbolic dream exploration, mini-games and painterly art.

Questions

Clear answers for players and families

What is Operation Sindoor?

It is an interactive narrative and visual novel following Ila through family moments, headlines and symbolic dream missions.

Is it a mythology game?

No. It sits under the Gamemithra Studios catalogue, but it is primarily a visual novel rather than a mythology or puzzle title.

Is it suitable for children?

The page describes a child protagonist and family storytelling, but also fear, conflict, news reports and national uncertainty. Adult guidance may be appropriate for younger children.

What gameplay is included?

The existing page mentions visual novel storytelling, dream-world investigation, educational quizzes, symbolic air missions and clue exploration.

What languages are supported?

Language support is not confirmed in the repository yet.

Who publishes it?

Operation Sindoor is developed and published by Gamemithra Studios.
