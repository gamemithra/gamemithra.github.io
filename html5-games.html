Animal Merge Puzzle

Zoo Merge Blitz

A fruit-merge style puzzle game rebuilt with animal heads.

Drop animal heads into the play area, merge matching animals, and keep the board under control as the chain grows. The game keeps the familiar merge-puzzle rhythm of fruit-merge games, but gives the board a playful zoo theme with animal sounds, bright feedback, and quick restart-friendly sessions.

Main HTML5 Game | Soccer Card Battle

Soccer Clash

Build your squad. Clash your cards. Control the match.

Soccer Clash is the main sports-card battle game in this collection. Players build a squad of football player cards, choose a deck, reveal matchups, and compete against an AI opponent through quick stat clashes and reflex-based challenges. Each round is built around fast decisions: compare player strengths, react to directional prompts, score attacking chances, defend AI pressure, and push for the win before momentum turns.

The game flow combines collectible card presentation with arcade match energy. Sports-card visuals, squad selection, animated reveals, round feedback, music, sound effects, and football-themed screens give Soccer Clash its own identity as the primary version of the clash formula.

Main game Football squad cards AI opponent battles Deck selection Stat-based clashes Quick reaction prompts Attack and defense flow Animated sports-card presentation
