For parents and educators

Play, Learn and Discover Indian Stories

Family-friendly games with cultural roots and thoughtful play.

Gamemithra Studios creates games that introduce younger audiences to Indian stories and cultural settings, encourage curiosity, combine play with knowledge, and support observation and problem-solving.

Trust Signals

What families should know

Intended audience

Family-oriented Gamemithra titles are intended for children, families, parents, teachers and culturally curious players.

Learning exposure

Ramayan Quest introduces Ramayana characters, stories, values and cultural vocabulary through quizzes and story progression.

Shared play

Parents can play Ramayan Quest with children and discuss the stories, symbols and choices that appear in the game.

Privacy

The privacy policy says Gamemithra does not collect personally identifiable information from users of its apps.

Ads and monetization

The privacy policy says Gamemithra apps use AdMob by Google to display ads. Specific monetization details per title should be confirmed on each store listing.

Adult guidance

Adult guidance may be useful for Operation Sindoor with younger children because the story includes fear, conflict, news reports and national uncertainty.
