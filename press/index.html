Facts

First-party studio reference

Studio

Gamemithra Studios is an independent Indian game studio based in Kozhikode, Kerala.

Positioning

Indian Stories. Interactive Worlds. The studio creates games inspired by mythology, culture, learning, puzzles, storytelling and family-friendly entertainment.

Contact

For press, publishing, education or collaboration inquiries, email studio@gamemithra.com.
