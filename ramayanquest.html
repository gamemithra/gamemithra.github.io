About the Game

More than a quiz. A journey through mythology.

Ramayan Quest transforms mythology learning into an interactive adventure experience.

Travel through iconic moments inspired by the Ramayana while answering quizzes, unlocking narrative scenes, discovering characters, and progressing through culturally inspired environments.

Designed for students, families, mythology enthusiasts, and casual mobile players, the game blends educational quiz gameplay, narrative progression, mythology-inspired storytelling, cinematic cut-scenes, accessible learning, and casual adventure mechanics.

The experience focuses on curiosity, discovery, and cultural storytelling rather than memorization alone.
