DWI Ludo

A familiar board game for family play.

Quick, friendly Ludo sessions designed for mobile and everyday players.

DWI Ludo brings a familiar board-style experience into a clean, colorful, mobile-friendly format for family members, friends, kids, and casual players.

About the Game

Simple rules, colorful play, familiar fun

DWI Ludo is part of Gamemithra Studios' Casual & Family Games collection. It focuses on recognisable board-game play, short sessions, approachable controls, and a warm visual style.

The game is intended for players who want an easy-to-understand casual experience that can be shared across family and friend groups.
