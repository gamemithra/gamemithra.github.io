About the studio

Gamemithra Studios

Indian Stories. Interactive Worlds.

Gamemithra Studios is an independent Indian game studio based in Kozhikode, Kerala, building Indian-themed story games, educational play, visual novels and mobile prototypes.

What Gamemithra creates

What is Gamemithra Studios?

Gamemithra Studios is an independent Indian game studio creating culturally rooted games and interactive experiences.

What kind of games does Gamemithra create?

The studio creates games inspired by Indian mythology, culture, learning, puzzles, storytelling, traditional games and family-friendly entertainment.

Which games does Gamemithra develop?

Gamemithra Studios develops and publishes Ramayan Quest, develops Puzzles of Vrindavan, and develops Operation Sindoor.

Who are the games for?

Primary audiences include parents, teachers, families, children, culturally curious players and players interested in Indian mythology and culture.
